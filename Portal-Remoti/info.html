Cast & Crew Information

Understanding the business, culture, and leadership.

Company Overview

Founded: 1976 | Headquarters: Burbank, California

Ownership: Private company, acquired by EQT in 2018.

Company Size: 3,000+ employees.

Cast & Crew is a leading entertainment technology and services company that supports film, television, streaming, live events, and other productions behind the scenes. The company manages many of the administrative and operational processes required to keep productions running smoothly, including payroll, production accounting, onboarding, contracts, time tracking, labor compliance, and tax incentives. Its goal is to simplify production operations so studios, production companies, and creative teams can focus on creating content.

Value Proposition & Culture

Mission

Cast & Crew aims to empower creativity by helping its customers succeed. The company works as a production partner, developing technology and workflows that make production processes more efficient and allow creative teams to focus on their work.

Core Services & Ecosystem

What Cast & Crew does

  • Payroll: Union and non-union payroll processing.
  • Production Accounting: Financial management, accounting tools, and reporting.
  • Onboarding & Compliance: Digital onboarding, tax documentation, and contracts.
  • Tax Incentives: Support identifying regional production incentives.
  • Labor Relations: Union compliance and contract interpretation.

Cast & Crew + Remoti Partnership

Remoti supports Cast & Crew by recruiting talent for multiple teams across the organization. The partnership allows Cast & Crew to expand its workforce by hiring professionals in Colombia (mainly Bogotá and Medellín) through Remoti.

  • Employment Model: Payroll through Remoti.
  • Working Hours: Must be available to work aligned with Pacific Time.
  • English Requirement: Advanced English (C1).
  • Benefits: 15 PTO days + most Colombian holidays.
