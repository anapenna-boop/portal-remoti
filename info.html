Company Overview

Founded: 1976 | Headquarters: Burbank, California

Ownership: Private company, acquired by EQT in 2018.

Company Size: 3,000+ employees.

Cast & Crew is a leading entertainment technology and services company that supports film, television, streaming, live events, and other productions behind the scenes. The company manages many of the administrative and operational processes required to keep productions running smoothly, including payroll, production accounting, onboarding, contracts, time tracking, labor compliance, and tax incentives. Its goal is to simplify production operations so studios, production companies, and creative teams can focus on creating content.
